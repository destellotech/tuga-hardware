# Outreach research: vendor listings, trade press and creators

Prepared 24 October 2026 for Tuga Hardware (Destello Tech Ltd). Scope: the 40 software vendors with "works with" pages on tugahardware.com, UK trade press that reviews tools and tech for trades, FM and field service, and UK trade creators on YouTube. Everything below came from web search results or pages fetched on the day. Where a page could not be opened (several sites block automated fetches) that is said in the Notes column, and the URL given is one that appeared in a search result, not a guess. "Not found" means a search for it returned nothing usable. Subscriber counts are from third party trackers (SponsorRadar, Modash, findachannel) and are rounded; they move weekly.

One general finding: none of the 40 vendors publishes a "recommended devices" or "certified hardware" page for rugged Android or Windows tablets. The realistic route is the technology or integration partner track, where a hardware vendor can be listed as a compatible or complementary solution. Two vendors (Limble and eMaint) name hardware manufacturers explicitly as eligible technology partners. Most UK vendors run a referral or reseller programme aimed at consultants and accountants, so the pitch has to be reframed as "listed compatible hardware plus a loan unit for your demo team" rather than "we want to resell your software".

Priority order for the founder: UK vendors with a partners@ mailbox first (BigChange, Joblogic, Totalmobile, Payaca, Tradify), then the two CMMS vendors that name hardware partners (Limble, eMaint), then the big marketplaces where listing is a form (Simpro, MaintainX, Planon, PlanRadar, Procore, Autodesk).
