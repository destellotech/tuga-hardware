Delivery

Please allow 7 to 10 working days from order for delivery.

We'll send you a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If your delivery takes longer than expected, contact us and we'll chase it down for you.

Free worldwide delivery

Every order qualifies for free standard delivery, wherever you are. No minimum order value, no hidden charges. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Duties and import taxes

We deliver to almost every country. Orders outside the UK are sent duties paid: any import duty or tax is paid before your order arrives, so the carrier will not ask you for money on delivery.

Paying in your currency

Prices on the site are in pounds sterling. Outside the UK, checkout shows the total in your own currency and charges you that amount, so you see exactly what you will pay before you pay it. The exchange rate is set by our payment provider, Stripe, and includes a small conversion margin. PayPal converts in the same way at its own rate.

Where we do not deliver

We do not deliver to Russia or Belarus, because of UK sanctions; to Afghanistan, Burkina Faso, the Central African Republic, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Haiti, Libya, Mali, Myanmar, Niger, the Palestinian Territories, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Venezuela or Yemen, where couriers cannot deliver safely; or to Ascension Island, the British Indian Ocean Territory, the Falkland Islands, Kiribati, Nauru, Niue, the Pitcairn Islands, Saint Helena, Svalbard and Jan Mayen, Tokelau, Tristan da Cunha, Tuvalu, Wallis and Futuna or Western Sahara, which no tracked courier from our supplier reaches. Checkout will not accept an address in those places; email us if you are unsure.

30 Day Return Policy

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Products must be in their original condition and packaging, with all accessories included.

Unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item, you pay the cost of returning it. If it is faulty or wrong and you bought as a consumer, we send you a prepaid label. Business buyers pay the carriage to send a faulty device to us and we pay to send it back, as set out in section 5a of our Terms. If we sent the wrong item, we pay both ways for everyone.

Outside the UK, returns and warranty repairs work the same way, except that you send the device back to us at your own cost, including any export paperwork or duty on that journey, and we pay to send a repaired or replacement device back to you. If we sent the wrong item, we still pay both ways.

How to Return an Item

  • Email us at support@tugahardware.com with your order number and reason for return.
  • We'll provide you with a returns authorisation and shipping instructions.
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging and ship it to the address provided.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund within 5 to 7 business days.

Damaged or Faulty Products

If your product arrives damaged or develops a fault within the warranty period, contact us immediately. We'll repair or replace it under the warranty, or refund you if we can do neither; see our warranty page for how a claim works and who pays carriage. Please include photos of the damage or fault when you get in touch, as this helps us resolve things faster.

Your statutory rights

On top of the 30 day policy above, the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 give you 14 days from delivery to cancel for any reason, and the Consumer Rights Act 2015 entitles you to a repair, replacement or refund if a product is faulty or not as described. These rights do not depend on the original packaging. If an order has not arrived within 30 days of payment, you can cancel it for a full refund.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order at any time before it ships. Once an order has been dispatched, you'll need to follow the standard returns process above. To cancel, email us with your order number and we'll confirm the cancellation.

Questions? Contact us at support@tugahardware.com and we'll get back to you within one business day.
