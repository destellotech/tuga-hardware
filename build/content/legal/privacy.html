Tuga Hardware ("we", "us", "our") operates the website tugahardware.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller

Destello Tech Ltd, trading as Tuga Hardware (company number 16595781, registered office 124 Vicarage Wood, Harlow, Essex CM20 3HH), is the data controller responsible for your personal data. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@tugahardware.com.

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you use our website or place an order:

  • Identity data: your name and title.
  • Contact data: your email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Transaction data: details of products you purchase, order history, and payment references.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and version, time zone, operating system, and platform.
  • Usage data: information about how you use our website, including pages visited and navigation paths.
  • Communication data: any correspondence you send us via email or the contact form.

We do not collect or store your full payment card details. All payment processing is handled securely by our third party payment providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfil your orders, including shipping and delivery.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and invoices.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To improve our website, products, and services.
  • To detect and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract: processing is necessary to fulfil your order and deliver products to you.
  • Legitimate interests: processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as improving our services and preventing fraud.
  • Consent: where you have given us consent, for example to receive marketing communications. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

5. Cookies and local storage

Our website sets no cookies unless you accept analytics cookies in the cookie notice. It keeps small items in your browser's local storage, which stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your basket: the items you have added, so your basket is still there when you come back.
  • This notice: your choice on analytics cookies, so the notice is not shown again.
  • Analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, and Google Analytics 4. Google Analytics only sets its cookies if you click Accept in the cookie notice; if you decline or ignore it, no cookies are set and Google receives only limited, cookieless signals. Advertising features and Google Signals are switched off. You can change your mind by clearing this site's storage in your browser.
  • Page counts: our server also keeps a daily count of how many times each page is opened, with the referring website, country and whether the device is a phone or a computer. It stores no IP address, cookie or anything else that could identify you.

You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings. Your basket will then be emptied. The payment pages run by Stripe and PayPal set their own cookies under their own policies.

6. Third Party Services

We share your data with trusted third parties only where necessary to provide our services:

  • Stripe: processes card payments securely. Stripe's privacy policy is available on their website.
  • PayPal: provides an alternative payment method. PayPal's privacy policy is available on their website.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website and stores orders, enquiries, trade applications, warranty registrations and reviews on our behalf.
  • Resend: sends our order, enquiry and review emails, so it receives the name, email address and order details in them.
  • Shipping providers: we share your name and delivery address with our logistics partners to fulfil your order.
  • Analytics providers: Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics 4 process aggregate page view data on our behalf. Google Analytics sets cookies only with your consent.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy. Order data is retained for a minimum of six years to comply with UK tax and accounting requirements. Contact form enquiries, newsletter sign ups and trade account applications are deleted automatically after 12 months unless you request earlier deletion. Warranty registrations and reviews are kept for as long as the device could be covered and for six years after, to deal with any claim.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: you can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: you can request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction: you can ask us to restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: you can request your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine readable format.
  • Objection: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@tugahardware.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology.
