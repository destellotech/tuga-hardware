Standardise on One Model

Give everyone the same device. The hardware is no better for it, but everything around it gets simpler:

  • One set of accessories. Whichever model you choose, the same car charger, screen protectors and mounts or docks fit every unit of that model, so you buy in multiples.
  • One setup. You work out the apps, accounts and settings once, write them down, and repeat them.
  • One spare. A single spare covers the whole team, and when someone leaves, is off sick or cracks a screen, any unit goes to any person.

When Two Models Make Sense

Some teams do have two shapes of job: drivers who need something in a pocket, and site staff who need a screen for forms. Then run two models, but keep them in the same family so the setup is as close as possible, and check your apps on both. In our range the Tuga A6 handheld (£299) and the Tuga A8 tablet (£379) both run Android 14 and both take the same 12V DC Car Charger. The same logic applies on Windows 11 with the Tuga WH6 handheld alongside the Tuga W8 or Tuga W10. Two models is the limit.

If everything the team uses runs in a browser or has an Android app, Android is the cheaper route, as long as you check each app on a trial unit first. Buy One First

Before you order for the team, order one. Give it to the person who will be hardest on it and run it on real jobs for a fortnight. Install every app the team uses and check each one runs on the device; do not assume. Keep the box and look after the unit: if it does not fit, you can return it within 30 days of delivery in its original condition and packaging, with all accessories, and you pay return postage unless it is faulty (conditions on our delivery and returns page). You have lost a fortnight and some postage, not a few thousand pounds.

There is a cost. The discount is worked out per order, so one unit now and four later earns less than five together: on the A8 the difference is about £76 across the five. If you already know the model, skip the trial. If not, £76 is cheap insurance.

How the Volume Discount Works

Our bulk orders page has a calculator, but the tiers are simple. The discount applies to devices only, is worked out on the total number of devices in the order, and comes off automatically at checkout:

  • 3 to 4 devices: 5 percent off

Set Every Device Up the Same Way

With two to ten devices you can usually manage without mobile device management (MDM) software; the enterprise guide explains why it only starts to pay at around 20 to 30 devices. You need a checklist, written on the trial device and followed on every unit:

  1. Number each device. A label on the back, and a list of serial numbers and who has which. Register the warranty as each one arrives, not when it breaks.
  2. SIM and data. The A6 has 5G, the A8, WH6 and W8 have 4G LTE, the A10 takes two SIMs, and the W10 has Wi-Fi and Ethernet with 4G LTE as an option. Order the SIMs before the devices arrive.
  3. One work account per person, not a shared login, so a lost device can be locked without locking everyone out.
  4. A screen lock on every unit. The A6 and W8 have a fingerprint sensor, the A6 adds face unlock, and the W10 offers a fingerprint reader as an option. On the others, a PIN.
  5. Install the apps in the same order, sign in, and set photos and documents to sync to wherever the business keeps them, so a device can be swapped without losing work.
  6. Do a pretend job on each device before handing it over.
