Our guide to the best rugged tablets for plumbers and heating engineers has the full reasoning. This is the short version: which one to buy.

What kills devices in plumbing and heating work

  • Water, all day. Wet hands, a drip from a joint, a tablet balanced on the edge of a bath or left on a wet floor under a sink.
  • Flux, jointing compound and boiler dust. It gets into the charging port and under the buttons, and it scratches glass when wiped off with a sleeve.
  • Airing cupboards, lofts and cylinder tops. Warm, cramped and dark. Anything balanced on a cylinder or a joist ends up on the floor.
  • Tiled floors. Kitchens and bathrooms have no soft landings. A drop from a worktop onto tiles finishes most consumer screens.
  • Van life. Summer sun on the dashboard, a cold start in January, the passenger seat between jobs.

What actually matters in a spec

Sealing rated for immersion. IP67 and IP68 are dust tight and rated for immersion, so a drop into a bath is within the rating. IP65 is not. Our IP68 vs IP67 guide explains what each number tests. None of our devices is rated IP69K, so wipe them clean and never jet wash them.

A camera with a flash. Data plates, flues, a joint before and after, the leak you want the customer to see, most of it in the dark. The A6, A8 and A10 each list a 13MP rear camera with a flash. The W8 lists an 8MP rear camera and no flash.

A battery for a day of callouts. A removable battery means a charged spare takes over on the last job. The A6 and A8 both have one.

Your software decides the operating system. Job management apps mostly run on Android or in a browser; our works-with pages compare what each vendor publishes as minimum requirements with the listed specs of each device. Boiler manufacturer and flue gas analyser apps are usually written for phones, so check the minimum Android version. Manufacturer diagnostic tools that connect through an interface lead are sometimes Windows only, and one of those settles it. Digital certificate software exists for this trade too; check with the provider that it runs on the device you are buying. Our Android or Windows guide has a five minute test.

A screen readable at the outside unit. At a heat pump or on a roof, brightness matters. The A8 is rated at 1000 nits.

Confirm with the manufacturer that the software runs on Windows 11, that the interface has a current driver and which connector it needs. The W8 is £250 more than the A8, its battery is smaller and not listed as removable, and its camera lists no flash. If the Windows program could run on the office PC after the job, buy Android and keep the difference. A version with a 2D barcode scanner is £699.

Questions plumbers and heating engineers ask

Will my boiler app and certificate software run on it?

If the app is available for Android and supports the version on the device, it should: Android 12 on the A8, Android 15 on the A6. Check the minimum version the app asks for and try it before you rely on it. If a program is Windows only, you need the W8. Check with the provider, or ask us if you are unsure.

Can I use it with wet hands?

The sealing covers the water. Neither the A6 nor the A8 lists a wet touch or glove mode, so expect to wipe the screen when water is sitting on it, and dry the device before you plug it in to charge.
